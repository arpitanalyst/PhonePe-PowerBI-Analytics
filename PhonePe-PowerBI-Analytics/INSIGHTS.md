# PhonePe Dashboard — Insights

## Executive Insights

### 1. Overall transaction performance
The Home page is built around transaction amount, transaction count,
successful transactions, and failed transactions. These KPIs provide a
high-level view of payment activity and operational performance.

### 2. Payment reliability
Success and failure status are tracked across the dashboard. Failure/reason
visuals make it possible to identify the categories that contribute most to
failed payments and prioritize investigation.

### 3. Time-based performance
Monthly trend visuals are present across the main service pages. This enables
analysis of growth, seasonality, sudden drops, and changes in transaction
activity over time.

### 4. Insurance performance
Insurance analysis focuses on premium value, payment status, reasons for
failed/unsuccessful payments, and insurance type. This can be used to compare
the contribution of different insurance categories.

### 5. Loan performance
The Loans page tracks total loan amount, payment status, monthly performance,
reasons, and loan type. This helps identify which loan categories contribute
more value and where payment issues occur.

### 6. Money transfer performance
The Money Transfer page compares transfer types, payment status, reasons,
monthly trends, and total transfer amount. This supports analysis of customer
transfer behavior and payment reliability.

### 7. Recharge & Bills performance
The Recharge & Bills page tracks total amount, recharge type, payment status,
reasons, and monthly trends. It can be used to identify high-value service
types and recurring failure categories.

## Recommended KPI Interpretation

When presenting this project in an interview, explain the KPIs in this order:

1. **Transaction Value** — total money processed.
2. **Transaction Volume** — number of transactions.
3. **Success Rate** — successful transactions as a share of total transactions.
4. **Failure Rate** — failed transactions as a share of total transactions.
5. **Failure Reasons** — categories causing unsuccessful transactions.
6. **Monthly Trend** — how performance changes over time.
7. **Service Mix** — which product/service types contribute more value.
